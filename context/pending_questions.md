# Blocking and nonblocking questions
Blocking before submission: official signing name; ownership percentage/control evidence; reconciled financials; tax filing status; current bank and debt records; program-specific eligibility; use-of-funds approval.
Blocking before accepting debt: repayment source; acceptable personal guarantee/collateral; total costs; downside forecast.
Needed for budgets: equipment quotes; workspace costs; business-trip frequency; transport alternatives; cash available; owner contribution.
Nonblocking for drafting: target funding amount, dedicated workspace preference, first customer segment.
Never request passwords or full bank/TIN values in chat. Use secure official channels for final application data.
