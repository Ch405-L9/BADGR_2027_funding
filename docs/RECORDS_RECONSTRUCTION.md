# Rebuilding 2025 business records — DRAFT
Updated 2026-10-08. Goal: document **real** 2025 business expenses and income after the fact. Use real records only. Never create, back-date or estimate a receipt to fill a gap. Private files go in `private/` (gitignored); log each item in `private/expense_records.csv` or `private/income_records.csv`.

## First: ask your tax preparer
- What records they used for the 2025 Schedule C (expense totals, the $19,704 depreciation asset list, the $455 car/truck figure).
- A copy of the depreciation schedule (Form 4562) and any workpapers. Those are your strongest backup.

## Where the missing receipts usually still exist
| Expense type (your list) | Where to recover it |
|---|---|
| USBs, SD cards, cleaner/duster, cabling, replacement screens, test equipment | Amazon / Best Buy / Walmart / eBay order history (each lets you re-download invoices); bank and card statements |
| Software, tool, GenAI and cloud subscriptions | Each vendor's billing page (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub, Google, Microsoft, Render); Google Play / Apple subscription history; email search for "receipt", "invoice", "payment" in 2025 |
| Travel gas | Card or bank statements show the station and amount. Business-use share needs a mileage log; rebuild one from calendar, texts and job invoices only if your preparer says that's acceptable |
| Customer income (repairs, installs, re-imaging, drone, marketing) | Bank deposits, Cash App / PayPal / Zelle / Venmo history, texts or emails with customers, any invoices |

## Rules
- A bank or card statement line plus an order-history page is a real record, even without the paper receipt.
- Mark anything you can't support as "unsupported". Don't guess an amount.
- Keep personal purchases out; mixed items need a business-use share your preparer agrees with.
- From now on: one business bank account, and an invoice for every job (your 2026 PC-repair invoice is a good model, but **don't write customers' device PINs on invoices**).

## Missing inputs
- The preparer's workpapers; 2025 bank/card statements; vendor order histories.
