## Short bio (about 75 words)
Anthony Grant is the founder of BADGRTECHNOLOGIES LLC, a Georgia LLC based in Lawrenceville, Gwinnett County.
Grant builds Python/FastAPI services, React web apps, Kotlin Android apps and Linux-based systems.
Grant's applied-AI work centers on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG, BM25, ChromaDB), MCP tooling and AI-agent workflow evaluation.
Before founding BADGR in January 2025, Grant spent nearly five years in Tier II/III technical support, implementation and field hardware engineering roles.

## Extended bio
Career path: technical support analyst and junior implementation manager (2019–2021), technical support engineer II (2021–2023), and field support hardware engineer II (2024–2025).
Grant completed six Meta course certificates through Coursera in Kotlin, Android UI, UX/UI design and version control (2023–2024).
Grant has completed 12 credit hours toward an Associate of Applied Science in Computer and Information Sciences at Northeast State Community College.
Grant's projects include a hybrid-retrieval system with evaluation and rollback controls (C.Walts), an agent-routing harness (BADGR Harness), and an Android reading app (BADGR Bolt).
