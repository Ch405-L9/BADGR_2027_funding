# DRAFT — Use-of-funds scenarios
Status: DRAFT, not owner-approved. Checked: 2026-10-08 (America/New_York). Planning scenarios, not funding requests.

## Rules
- Each tier is a planning scenario, not a request.
- Every line must fit the chosen program's allowed uses; for example, NASE excludes debt and rent, and SBA microloans exclude real estate and existing debt.
- Equipment bought in 2025 is excluded; every amount needs a written quote.
- No line may be funded by two sources.

| Tier | Categories (owner selects) |
