## Narrative
BADGR Technologies is a Black-owned, one-person technology company in Lawrenceville, Gwinnett County, founded in January 2025.
It aims to give small local organizations the kind of automation and careful AI adoption that larger firms get from in-house IT staff.
The founder learned this work through years of hands-on support and implementation roles and through self-directed technical courses.
Planned impact measures are the number of local organizations served, hours of manual work automated for them, and revenue retained in the local economy; targets will be set with the owner.
The company plans to use free local business support (SBDC, Gwinnett Entrepreneur Center) as part of its growth plan.
