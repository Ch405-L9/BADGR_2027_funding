## Original guide
Daily: 10 minutes SAM/validation requests; 30 minutes records; 45 minutes funding task; 30 minutes customer work; 5 minutes tracker.
1. Confirm facts and sanitize sources.
2. Request SBDC/APEX and workspace assistance.
3. Equipment inventory and quotes.
4. Verizon prerequisites and fresh rule check.
5. Reconcile financial records.
6. Workspace and transport comparison.
7. Cash-flow and repayment review.
8. Kiva restrictions/supporter screening.
9. Microloan/CDFI preliminary fit calls.
10. Submit only reviewed, complete applications yourself.
11–15. Follow-ups, source checks, one R&D concept and revised customer pipeline.
16–30. Repeat weekly review, act on actual responses, update forecasts, refresh sources before submissions.
SAM activation inserts CAGE/final record/expiration verification and Grants.gov/agency role checks; does not award funds.
